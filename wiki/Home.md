# CAN Claims

**CAN Claims** is a comprehensive land claiming, city management, and diplomacy mod for [Vintage Story](https://www.vintagestory.at/). It adds a full-featured territorial governance system with cities, alliances, warfare, economy, and more.

## Key Features

- **Cities** — Create and manage cities with citizens, ranks, permissions, and territory
- **Land Claims** — Claim plots (16x16 chunks) to protect builds from griefing
- **Alliances** — Form alliances between cities for shared benefits and diplomacy
- **War & Conflicts** — Declare wars, schedule battles, capture enemy territory with battle flags
- **Economy** — City treasuries, daily maintenance costs, plot fees, and trade integration
- **Prison System** — Imprison enemy players with ransom mechanics
- **Permissions** — Fine-grained access control for plots, cities, and groups
- **Chat Channels** — City, local, and global chat with colored prefixes
- **Summon/Teleportation** — Set up teleport points within your city
- **Plot Groups** — Organize plots into groups with shared permissions
